How to play
  • Steer with the mouse (or WASD / arrows, or drag on touch).
  • Collect Influence, Campaign Donations, Budget Bonuses, Public Contracts and briefcases to grow.
  • Boost with Space or hold the left mouse button — it burns a little mass.
  • Special ability: E or right-click. Each politician has a different one.
  • If your head touches another politician’s body, your career is over. Make rivals crash into you and collect their spilled funds.
  • Don’t leave the country — the tricolour border is fatal. P/Esc pauses, M mutes.

Fictional political parody. All characters are invented archetypes; rivals are computer-controlled AI, not real players.
